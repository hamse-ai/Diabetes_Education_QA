# MedQuAD candidate review queue

`medquad_review_queue.csv` contains de-duplicated exact question/answer pairs from the first-pass general-adult scope filter. It is an annotation queue, not an approved training dataset. Repeated question-answer pairs are collapsed while source URLs and source collections are retained.

For each record, fill in:

- `scope_decision`: `include` or `exclude` for general adult diabetes education.
- `source_support_review`: `supported`, `unsupported`, or `unclear` after checking the linked source.
- `currentness_review`: `current`, `dated`, or `unclear`.
- `safety_review`: `safe_general_education`, `needs_rewrite`, or `exclude`.
- `approved_for_training`: `yes` only after scope and evidence checks pass; otherwise `no`.
- `reviewer_notes`: explain exclusions, contradictions, missing source context, or needed rewrite.

The previous automated `scope_review` value is only a rough label. Review references and do not present the data as clinically validated. Preserve source attribution and follow the upstream CC BY 4.0 terms.


## Triage and held-out evaluation draft

`medquad_triage.csv` adds rule-based screening flags to the queue; reproduce it with `python src/triage_medquad.py`. These flags help prioritize review but do not set human review fields or approve training data.

`evaluation_set_draft.csv` is a separate 15-question draft with topic/source references and expected behavior. It is not part of training. The labels need independent relevance and safety review before it is frozen as a gold evaluation set.

`evaluation_challenge_set.csv` adds 15 hand-authored paraphrase, open-domain, personalized-care, urgent-boundary, and unsupported-subdomain prompts. The saved Run 003 rankings, recorded app routing, and preliminary author-led judgments are in `reports/evaluation_challenge_predictions.csv`. These prompts and judgments are exploratory and are not a gold benchmark.

`medquad_scope_screen.csv` is a reproducible scope-only draft generated by `python src/screen_medquad_scope.py`. It tentatively excludes rare-disease records, an American Indian-specific guide, and US financial/insurance guidance. The source review and all training approvals remain pending.

`source_document_review.csv` groups the 175 Q/A candidates by their 42 source pages, so provenance, currentness, and broad scope can be checked once per page before reviewing individual answer pairs. Generate it with `python src/build_source_review_sheet.py`. Page-level checks do not replace answer-level support and safety review.
